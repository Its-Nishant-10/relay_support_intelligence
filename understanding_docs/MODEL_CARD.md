# Model Card: Relay Support Intelligence

## Model Purpose

Predict the intake category, priority, routing queue, and sentiment of a customer-support ticket to assist operations teams.

## Training Dataset and Provenance

Category, priority, and queue models use `Prady06/customer-support-tickets` from Hugging Face. Its dataset card describes it as a **synthetic IT ticket dataset**, so this project does not describe it as real customer or company data. Sentiment uses `SetFit/sst2`, a public Stanford Sentiment Treebank-derived dataset accessed through Hugging Face. Run `python scripts/download_dataset.py` to reproduce the download.

## Features and Targets

Features are limited to `subject + body`, the information available at ticket intake. Targets are the dataset's `type`, `priority`, and `queue` fields. The sentiment target is the public sentiment dataset's label.

## Algorithms and Split

Two TF-IDF candidates are compared for each target: Logistic Regression and LinearSVC. The winner is selected by held-out macro F1. The split is 80% train and 20% test, stratified with `random_state=42`. Selected pipelines are saved under `models/`.

## Evaluation

Actual metrics are generated by `python scripts/train.py` and saved in `reports/metrics.json`. Confusion matrices are saved under `reports/figures/`. No metric is hardcoded in the app.

## Known Limitations and Potential Biases

The support dataset is synthetic, may not represent a particular company's vocabulary, and may contain class imbalance or artifacts. Model confidence can be overconfident on domain-shifted tickets. Queue labels may reflect the dataset's internal taxonomy rather than a user's organization. Sentiment trained on general sentiment text may not transfer perfectly to support language. English-only or multilingual behavior depends on the downloaded labels and training distribution.

## Intended Use

Decision support for triage experiments, portfolio demonstration, and offline evaluation with appropriately reviewed data.

## Out-of-Scope Use

Do not use this system as the sole decision-maker for customer outcomes, account closures, security incidents, refunds, or compliance actions. Do not treat synthetic-data metrics as production performance.

## Human Review Policy

Tickets below the configurable 0.50 default confidence threshold are marked `HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED`. Confidence is the mean of the four calibrated model probabilities. Keyword and category business policies can also require review independently. Agents should verify the model output before taking consequential action.
